Guidelines for CDS Contact with Civic Donors

When approaching registered NGOs, always attach an endorsed letter of introduction carrying the official NYSC Secretariat stamp signed by your CDS Schedule Officer and Local Government Inspector (LGI). Do not accept direct cash payments; all monetary endorsements must flow through designated state CDS bank instruments.

REQUISITION FOR CORPORATE CSR PARTNERSHIP: DONATION OF 2,500 SANITARY HYGIENE PACKS FOR PUBLIC SECONDARY SCHOOL GIRLS IN BENIN METROPOLIS (SDG 3 & 5)

Dear Sir/Madam,

The National Youth Service Corps (NYSC) Edo State Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs) Community Development Service (CDS) Group respectfully presents this urgent requisition for community partnership under our flagship “Dignity for the Girl-Child Project”.

Our grassroots baseline assessment conducted in September 2024 revealed that over 43% of teenage adolescent girls across municipal schools in Ikpoba-Okha and Oredo LGAs experience periodic absenteeism due to lack of access to hygienic menstrual protection products and adequate reproductive wellness guidance.

PROJECT IMPACT TARGETS:

  • Direct distribution of 2,500 sanitary pad packages across 10 municipal secondary schools.
  • Free adolescent reproductive hygiene and peer empowerment workshops facilitated alongside qualified NYSC medical doctors and GPI Nigeria facilitators.
  • Establishment of permanent sanitary emergency cabinets in recipient school guidance offices.

We formally request your esteemed organization to donate in-kind sanitary packages (or grant financial support of NGN 1,850,000) toward product procurement and logistical deployment. Corporate sponsors will receive prime brand placement on all campaign media, state NYSC press releases, and a certificate of social investment issued by the NYSC State Coordinator.
