gavel How are project funds audited and reconciled?
expand_more

Every withdrawal requires simultaneous physical counter-signing by the Corps Member President, CDS Financial Secretary, and the NYSC Local Government Inspector (Schedule Officer). Monthly balance sheets are posted directly to this portal and read aloud during Thursday CDS meetings at Oredo LGA grounds.

inventory Can organizations donate physical materials instead of cash?
expand_more

Yes. For initiatives like the Sanitary Pads Drive or Market Sanitation, we actively encourage direct shipments of sealed sanitary pads, wheeled waste bins, high-visibility vests, and shovels. Material donors receive an official NYSC Certificate of Commendation and acknowledgment in press releases.

handshake How do local schools and market associations verify your team?
expand_more

All corps members wear official NYSC khaki uniforms and display valid federal ID cards alongside State Coordinator introductory dispatches when entering schools like Idia College or Emotan College. Our school engagement dates are pre-registered with the Edo State Post Primary Education Board (PPEB).
