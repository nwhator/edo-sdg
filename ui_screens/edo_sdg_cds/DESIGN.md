## Brand & Style

The design system embodies modern civic authority, grassroots communal dynamism, and institutional pride. Grounded in the patriotic heritage of the National Youth Service Corps (NYSC) and propelled by the United Nations Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs), the aesthetic balances federal prestige with youth-led community transformation across Edo State.

The emotional tone is reliable, catalytic, and welcoming. Interfaces communicate order, purpose, and measurable impact through high-clarity typography, crisp structural cards, and purposeful national color accents.

The visual style blends **Corporate Modern** structure with **Civic Vitality**:
- Clean, open layouts anchored by crisp slate canvas backgrounds.
- High-contrast structural cards framed in delicate borders rather than heavy drop shadows.
- Vivid contextual accents representing real-world initiatives: sanitary pad distributions, clean market interventions, and municipal school outreach across Benin City.
- Crisp utilitarian information density paired with approachable geometric sans-serif typefaces.

## Colors

The palette establishes institutional credibility through the historic NYSC green and gold, flanked by clean administrative neutrals and vibrant thematic hues for active community projects.

### Palette Hierarchy & Intent

- **Primary (Forest Green - #006633):** The lead color for administrative identity, primary actions, and navigational anchors. Represents state service, agriculture, and nationwide youth impact.
  - Hover: `#008744`
  - Active/Deep Pine: `#004724`
  - Midnight Green: `#002E17` for high-contrast footer surfaces and text anchors.
  - Mint Wash: `#F0FDF4` for selected item highlights and positive metric fills.
- **Secondary (NYSC Gold - #F4C430):** Represents leadership, excellence, and civic honors. Utilized for status alerts, badges, featured volunteer accolades, and call-to-action highlights. Supported by Warm Amber (`#B45309`) for text contrast and Khaki Sand (`#C3B091`) for badge borders or subdued structural dividers.
- **Cause Accent Palettes:**
  - *Sanitary Pads & Maternal Welfare:* Crimson Rose (`#E11D48`) base with Rose Wash (`#FFF1F2`) backgrounds for health metrics, sanitary kit tracking, and donation drives.
  - *Market Sanitation & Environmental Action:* Aqua Teal (`#0D9488`) base with Teal Wash (`#F0FDFA`) backgrounds for clean-up rosters, public hygiene campaigns, and SDG 6/11 indicators.
- **Neutrals & Surfaces:**
  - Base canvas is Slate-50 (`#F8FAFC`), providing an airy backdrop that lets clean white cards (`#FFFFFF`) framed by Slate-200 (`#E2E8F0`) stand out with optical clarity.
  - Text maintains high legibility: Headings leverage Slate-900 (`#0F172A`) for authoritative hierarchy, while long-form body content uses Slate-700 (`#334155`).

## Typography

The type system blends the human, modern geometry of **Plus Jakarta Sans** with the utilitarian precision of **Inter**.

- **Display & Headings (Plus Jakarta Sans):** Chosen for its energetic civic character and crisp terminals. Used for all main section titles, campaign counters, call-outs, and dashboard KPIs. Its geometric construction ensures Edo CDS impact numbers stand out with authority.
- **Body & Paragraphs (Inter):** Chosen for supreme legibility across dense project reports, CDS attendance registers, campaign logs, and meeting minutes. Maintains high readability even under harsh outdoor sunlight on mid-range mobile devices.
- **Labels & Microcopy (Plus Jakarta Sans):** Employs medium-to-bold weights with slight positive letter spacing to establish quick visual anchors for badges, status chips, form labels, and table metadata.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built upon an adaptive 12-column fluid grid system bounded by maximum container widths for structured, readable presentations.

### Grid Breakpoints
- **Mobile (< 640px):** 4-column layout with `1rem` outer margins and `1rem` gutters. Content cascades vertically; tabular data converts to structured summary cards.
- **Tablet (640px - 1024px):** 8-column layout with `2rem` outer margins and `1.5rem` gutters. Metric cards sit in 2x2 grids; primary operational tables maintain full width.
- **Desktop (> 1024px):** 12-column layout with max-width capped at `1280px` (or `1440px` on wide panels). `3rem` margins and `2rem` gutters deliver disciplined civic symmetry.

### Spacing Rhythm
Spacing adheres to a strict 4px/8px incremental rhythm:
- Use `space-xs` (4px) and `space-sm` (8px) for internal element grouping, badge padding, and icon-to-label separation.
- Use `space-md` (16px) for interior card padding, standard input field padding, and compact stack gaps.
- Use `space-lg` (24px) for desktop card padding, section gaps within cards, and column separation.
- Use `space-xl` (32px) through `space-3xl` (64px) for page section division, landing hero buffers, and major civic dashboard milestones.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy is maintained primarily through structured borders and subtle ambient layering rather than heavy drop shadows, reinforcing a crisp, clean civic aesthetic.

### Surface System
- **Level 0 (Canvas):** Pure `#F8FAFC` base surface for page backgrounds and administrative body screens.
- **Level 1 (Default Containers):** `#FFFFFF` surfaces bounded by a crisp 1px solid border of `#E2E8F0`. Elevation is supplemented with a feather-light ambient shadow: `0 1px 3px 0 rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.05), 0 1px 2px -1px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.05)`.
- **Level 2 (Interactive & Hovered Cards):** `#FFFFFF` surface accompanied by a subtle lift: `0 4px 6px -1px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.07), 0 2px 4px -2px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.05)`, with the border transitioning to `#CBD5E1`.
- **Level 3 (Modals, Overlays, Floating Menus):** Pure white with a deliberate ambient shadow tinted with Midnight Green: `0 20px 25px -5px rgba(0, 46, 23, 0.08), 0 8px 10px -6px rgba(0, 46, 23, 0.04)`.
- **Tonal Inset Surfaces:** `#F0FDF4` for green initiatives, `#FFF1F2` for sanitary causes, and `#F0FDFA` for water/sanitation programs, framed with matching 50% opacity borders.

## Shapes

The design system uses a balanced, contemporary rounded corner scale (`level 2` - 0.5rem base) that bridges administrative stability with modern youth-oriented software design.

- **Standard Elements (0.5rem / 8px):** Input inputs, default buttons, select menus, table card wrappers, and operational chips.
- **Large Cards & Modules (1rem / 16px):** Campaign summary cards, project statistics cards, modal sheets, and hero banners.
- **Hero & Feature Tiles (1.5rem / 24px):** Impact highlight features, spotlight community galleries, and call-to-action clusters.
- **Full Pill (`9999px`):** Status indicator tags, SDG goal identifier chips, and round avatar/icon enclosures.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Solid NYSC Forest Green (`#006633`) background with `#FFFFFF` text. Hover shifts to `#008744`; active state settles into Deep Pine (`#004724`). Border radius is 8px. Text style is `label-lg`.
- **Secondary / Civic Accent:** Solid NYSC Gold (`#F4C430`) with Slate-900 (`#0F172A`) text for high-priority calls to action, donations, and volunteer mobilization. Hover shifts to Warm Amber (`#B45309`) with `#FFFFFF` text.
- **Outline:** `#FFFFFF` background, 1.5px solid Forest Green (`#006633`) border, `#006633` text. Hover adds Mint Wash (`#F0FDF4`) fill.
- **Subtle / Ghost:** Slate-700 (`#334155`) text with no default border. Hover fills with `#F1F5F9`.

### Chips & Badges
- **General CDS Status:** Rounded pill (`9999px`), 4px vertical / 10px horizontal padding.
  - Active / Present: Mint Wash (`#F0FDF4`) background with Deep Pine (`#004724`) text and 1px `#BBF7D0` border.
  - Pending / Review: Gold tint (`#FEF9C3`) background with Amber (`#B45309`) text.
- **Cause Badges:**
  - *Sanitary Pads Campaign:* `#FFF1F2` background, `#E11D48` text, 1px `#FECDD3` border.
  - *Market Sanitation:* `#F0FDFA` background, `#0D9488` text, 1px `#99F6E4` border.

### Cards
- **Base Card:** Pure white (`#FFFFFF`) surface, 1px `#E2E8F0` border, 16px border-radius, and 24px interior padding (`space-lg`).
- **Campaign Impact Card:** Features a 4px solid top indicator border matching the cause accent (e.g., `#E11D48` for sanitary pads, `#0D9488` for market sanitation, `#006633` for CDS administration). Displays target progress bars, local Benin City venue metadata, and volunteer tallies.

### Form Inputs & Selects
- Background is `#FFFFFF`, border is 1px solid `#CBD5E1`, with 8px radius.
- Inactive text uses `#334155`, placeholder uses `#94A3B8`.
- Focus state activates a 2px outline in Forest Green (`#006633`) with a soft glow ring (`rgba(0, 102, 51, 0.15)`).
- Error state switches border to `#E11D48` with helper text in Crimson.

### Selection Controls (Checkboxes & Radios)
- Unchecked: 1.5px `#94A3B8` border with `#FFFFFF` fill.
- Checked: Forest Green (`#006633`) fill with white checkmark or center pip.
- Radio buttons retain a full circular silhouette (`9999px`), while checkboxes use a soft 4px radius.

### Lists & Activity Feeds
- CDS executive logs and volunteer roll-calls utilize alternate subtle row striping or 1px bottom dividers in `#F1F5F9`.
- Each list item includes an avatar or campaign pill, participant NYSC State Code (e.g., `ED/24B/...`), timestamp, and assigned task.

### Domain-Specific Components
- **State Code Identifier Badge:** Monospaced-style tag utilizing Khaki Sand (`#C3B091`) background with `#002E17` bold typography, identifying corps members and batch hierarchies.
- **SDG Target Metric Bar:** Two-tone progress track with neutral `#E2E8F0` base, filled dynamically with Cause-specific colors (Teal or Crimson), displaying Benin City local government areas (e.g., Oredo, Ikpoba Okha, Egor) reaching goal milestones.
